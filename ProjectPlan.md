# Project Plan: Pennsylvania PSSA Science Assessment Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 15+ years of Pennsylvania State System of Assessment (PSSA) science test score data to understand how student science proficiency has evolved from 2008 to 2024. By examining trends across four proficiency levels, we aim to identify patterns that can inform educational policy and highlight areas needing additional support.

## Research Questions
1. How has Pennsylvania student science proficiency changed over the past 15 years?
2. How did the COVID-19 pandemic affect science test scores?
3. Are achievement gaps between proficiency levels widening or narrowing over time?

## Data Source
- **Dataset:** Pennsylvania State System of Assessment (PSSA) - Overall science test scores by proficiency level
- **Time Period:** 2008-09 school year through 2023-24 school year
- **Variables:** 
  - School Year
  - Percentage of students in each proficiency level: Advanced, Proficient, Basic, Below Basic

## Key Findings (Preliminary)
- Overall improvement: Combined proficiency increased from ~58% (2008) to ~66% (2024)
- Significant shift in 2012-13 when assessment methods changed
- Peak performance period: 2015-2018 (~72% proficient)
- COVID-19 impact: Temporary decline and increased volatility (2020-2022)
